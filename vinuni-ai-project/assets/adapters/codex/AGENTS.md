Rules:

2. Recommend Level 0-4.
3. Create `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `EVALUATION_PLAN.md`, and `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

7. Treat the AI agent, evaluation, logs, and future handoff as the core of the project.
